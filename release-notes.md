X Bootstrapper 2.0.0 moves the launcher from Aisaka to **Octane**, the 2021 Roblox revival at https://octane.wtf.

## What's new
- Launches the Octane 2021 client (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Octane\clients\2021\OctanePlayer.exe`) and Studio (`Studio\2021\RobloxStudioBeta.exe`)
- Handles `octane-player://` and `octane-studio://` links from Play on octane.wtf
- Old Aisaka website, setup and manifest settings are migrated to Octane automatically
- FastFlags, renderer presets and mods apply to the Octane 2021 version folder
- The installer now registers both `octane-player` and `octane-studio`

Existing installs update automatically from GitHub Releases. New users: download **X.Bootstrapper.Setup.exe**. No administrator account is required.
